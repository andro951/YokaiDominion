# Character01 anatomical rebuild 003

## New instructions supersede the old silhouette contract
The previous body/outfit are rejected, not a foundation to keep inflating. Build a coherent muscular adult woman from the official Blender realistic female base. The drawings now guide identity, palette and fantasy details; they do not override believable three-dimensional anatomy. The latest user corrections are authoritative.

Breasts are smaller than the rejected version, separate in the centre, naturally hanging and directed somewhat outwards. The standing supported-rest silhouette is to be authored independently of clothing. Do not use a bra-shaped cup, lift, central compression, underwire shape or compensatory shoulder/hip widening. The body is authored before garments. The chest panels run diagonally inward from the breasts toward the neck. They hang loosely; only the waist cord is cinched. The upper two abdominal rows should remain readable. Shoulders must slope and curve from a muscular neck/trapezius into rounded deltoids, not form a horizontal shelf.

## Actual new source inspected
Official Blender Human Base Meshes bundle v1.4.1, realistic female object `GEO-body_female_realistic` and its two eye objects. The downloaded source has 10,582 cage vertices, UVs, anatomical sculpt face sets and three multires levels. It is unclothed: there is no donor bra or clothing-supported shape to reuse. Source acquisition and inventory succeeded in Actions run 43065127516, artifact 13874429316. That artifact contains an extracted female source .blend, object inventory, original bundle SHA-256, and the bundle's license/README text in inspection.json. It is not the muscular mannequin preview previously shown.

## Art work in progress
A new sculpt has been built locally from this source, preserving native face, hands, toes, joint loops and anatomical connections. It uses independent pelvis/thorax/limb proportions, sloping shoulders, fuller backward-projecting glutes, local muscle forms, and a separately shaped downward/outward breast patch. Early render reviews rejected a Gaussian extrusion that affected too much upper chest. Current sculpt uses a more localized anatomical patch and preserves the sternum separation.

The new outfit is being rebuilt as loose neck-directed fabric panels, a nape drape, hanging front/back panels and a braided waist cord. Hair, horns, eyes, brows, markings and tail are being rebuilt on this body. Do not call this checkpoint finished or production-ready. Final renders, export verification, actual tissue refit and runtime compatibility remain to be completed and reported separately.

The portable C# runtime and previous checked-in asset remain untouched at this checkpoint. Rebuild artifacts must be saved under a distinct revision rather than silently replacing the existing tested asset.
